## The 5 Components of a Working Outbound Sales System (and Where Most Fail)

Every working outbound system has five components. Most agencies have one or two. The agencies generating consistent pipeline have all five connected:

1. **Lead research and list building.** Identifying the right companies and contacts before any outreach begins. Tools: [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io), [Clay](https://www.clay.com), [LinkedIn Sales Navigator](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions).
2. **Cold calling with a power dialer.** High-volume phone outreach with automatic progression through a call list. Tool: GoHighLevel power dialer or [Kixie](https://www.kixie.com).
3. **Cold email sequences.** Multi-step email sequences sent to prospects who are not ready for a call or did not answer. Tools: GoHighLevel email workflows, [Instantly.ai](http://Instantly.ai), or [Smartlead](https://smartlead.ai) for dedicated cold email infrastructure.
4. **Automated follow-up.** Every touchpoint (call, email open, reply, no-show) triggers the next action automatically. Tool: GoHighLevel workflows.
5. **Pipeline and reporting.** Visual pipeline tracking plus weekly metrics on calls made, emails sent, meetings booked, and conversion rates. Tool: GoHighLevel pipelines and dashboards.

Where most agencies fail is between components. They have a dialer but no follow-up automation. They send cold emails but do not connect replies to a pipeline. They make calls but do not track outcomes. The system only works when all five components are connected and data flows automatically from one to the next.

## Cold Email + Cold Calling: How to Run Both Without Burning Out Your Team

The highest-performing outbound systems combine phone and email in a coordinated sequence. Here is the pattern that works for Australian agencies:

**Day 1:** Cold email lands in the prospect's inbox. Short, personalised, no attachments. Introduces the agency and asks a single question relevant to the prospect's business.

**Day 3:** If no reply, a second email follows up with a brief case study or data point. Still no hard pitch.

**Day 5:** Cold call. The caller references the emails: "I sent you a note earlier this week about [topic]. Wanted to see if it resonated." This warm-up dramatically improves cold call receptiveness.

**Day 5 (post-call):** If connected, the GHL workflow triggers based on call disposition. If no answer, a voicemail drop and an SMS follow-up go out automatically.

**Day 10:** Final email in the sequence. Direct ask for a 15-minute call. If no engagement across all touchpoints, the contact moves to a long-cycle nurture drip.

This coordinated approach means prospects hear from you across multiple channels without any manual effort beyond the phone call itself. The emails, SMS messages, voicemails, and pipeline updates all run on GHL workflows.

For AEST timing, schedule cold emails to land between 7:30am and 8:30am (caught early in the inbox), and cold calls between 9:30am and 11:30am or 2pm and 4pm for highest connection rates.
